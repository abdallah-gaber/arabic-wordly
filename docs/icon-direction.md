# Icon Direction

## Selected Concept

A rounded jade app icon with five cream word tiles across the center line. The final tile is highlighted in amber and carries a bold Arabic `و` to connect the icon to Arabic letter play while still reading clearly at small sizes.

## Rationale

- Feels close to the game board without copying the full screen UI.
- Uses the same core palette as the app for a more coherent product identity.
- The highlighted final tile creates a memorable silhouette.
- The Arabic letter makes the icon feel language-specific instead of generic.
